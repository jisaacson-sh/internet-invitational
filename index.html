Internet Invitational 2026: The 64 Players

All 64 players by drafted team, with a short profile, handicap, and follower count. Rosters only; nothing about matches.

Notes. Rosters come from handicap-tracker.com/events/internet-invitational-2026. Handicap indexes come from a GHIN Golfer Lookup on Nov 4, 2026, matched by name only, so a single match is very likely but not certain to be the same person. A plus sign means better than scratch. Players not found may have private GHIN profiles. Profiles marked "not verified" or "Unknown" couldn't be confirmed. Photos come from ytgolfstats.com/creators (a few from the players' public X, YouTube or Wikimedia Commons profile pictures) and are embedded in this file; a player without a photo shows initials. Click any player for a larger photo and their full details. Estimates marked (ytgolfstats) are that site's handicap estimates from tracked rounds. Follower counts are as of Nov 4, 2026, taken from each player's biggest public account (YouTube, Instagram or X), rounded to K/M; "—" means no count could be found.
